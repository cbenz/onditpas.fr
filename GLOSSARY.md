# onditpas.fr

A static site of one-page rants against a misused word or expression, each
reachable at `onditpas.fr/{slug}`.

## Language

**Forme fautive**:
The form people wrongly write or say. It is the slug of the page and the subject of the rant — `publique` for `onditpas.fr/publique`, even though `publique` is correct French elsewhere.
_Avoid_: erreur, faute d'orthographe

**Forme correcte**:
What should be said instead of the forme fautive. Optional: some fiches have none, and say so.
_Avoid_: correction, bonne orthographe

**Fiche**:
One page, addressed by its forme fautive, covering a single rant. Has at least one registre.
_Avoid_: article, page, entrée, erreur

**Registre**:
The tone a fiche is written in. `deadpan` is the plain one at `/{slug}`; `bordel` is the shouted one at `/{slug}/bordel`.
_Avoid_: ton, version, variante
